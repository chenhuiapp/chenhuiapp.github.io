Chenhui Spot Mail

Spot is a small, keyboard-first Gmail client for macOS, built by its developer for personal use.

What it does

  • Shows your Gmail inbox, with To do and Reminders lists for triage.
  • Reads, replies to, forwards, archives and labels mail when you ask it to.
  • Shows a read-only week view of your Google Calendar.
  • Writes mail in Markdown, with signatures and keyboard shortcuts.

How it works

Spot runs on your Mac and talks directly to Google's Gmail and Calendar APIs. There is no Spot server: your mail goes between your Mac and Google only.

Availability

Spot is not distributed publicly. It is used by its developer.
