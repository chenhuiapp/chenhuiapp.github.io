Chenhui Spot Mail

Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026.

Chenhui Spot Mail ("Spot") is a Gmail client for macOS. It runs on your Mac and connects directly to Google. There is no Spot server, so your data is never sent to the developer.

If you turn on its Claude features, Spot also connects to Anthropic, the maker of Claude, as described below.

Google data Spot accesses

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): to list and show your messages, send mail you write, and archive, trash or label messages when you ask, and, if you turn on appointments and deadlines, to read each new email on your Mac for a date, and to label an email whose appointment Spot added, changed or removed Spot/Booked and take it out of your inbox until that time has passed, and, whatever the appointments setting says, to read an email you ask to turn into an event and label it the same way, and, while Accept invitations is on (it is unless you turn it off), to read each new email in full on your Mac to find a calendar invitation, and to label one Spot said Yes to, added, or found you had already accepted Spot/Booked the same way. Spot creates three Gmail labels, Spot/To do, Spot/Reminder and Spot/Booked, to keep your triage state.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly, calendar.events): to show your calendars and events, to answer an invitation when you press Yes, Maybe or No, or, while Accept invitations is on, to say Yes to a new invitation from someone you know that you haven't answered (Google then tells the organizer), and to add events you create in Spot or invitations you ask it to add, or, with Accept invitations on, one from someone you know that isn't in your calendar (for an invitation that asks for an answer, only once it still isn't there 10 minutes later). Spot changes your calendar only when you ask, or, if you turn on Add automatically, to add, update or remove events it made from appointment emails, or, while Accept invitations is on, to answer or add invitations from people you know. It never changes calendar settings or sharing.
  • Google Contacts (contacts.readonly, contacts.other.readonly): to suggest addresses as you type in To, Cc, Bcc and an event's guests, from your contacts and the "Other contacts" Google saves when you email someone, and, while Accept invitations is on, to tell whether an invitation comes from someone you know (in your contacts, or someone you have emailed). Spot only reads them; it never changes your contacts.
  • Basic profile (openid, email): to show which accounts are signed in.

What Spot stores

  • Mail, calendar and contacts data are fetched from Google when needed and kept in memory while Spot runs.
  • On your Mac, Spot stores your sign-in token encrypted with the macOS Keychain, plus a settings file with your settings, signatures, reminder times, the send times of emails you schedule, the addresses you send mail to (with how often and when, read once from your recent Sent mail and then from what you send in Spot, to order suggestions and to tell whether an invitation comes from someone you know), addresses you asked Spot not to suggest (they still count as people you know), the appointments and deadlines found in your emails (their titles, times, places and short notes such as a confirmation number or contact phone, and the calendar changes Spot made for them; kept 60 days after the email arrived, or until their dates pass if that is later; those of an email you asked about with ⌃⌥⇧⌘E, at least 60 days after you last asked), when each account was last checked for them, for Accept invitations when each account was last checked, which invitation emails Spot already handled (kept 8 days) and when the meeting of each invitation Spot handled ends (kept until 7 days after the meeting), the last 10 instructions you typed for ⌘J rewrites, and the text of drafts you write in Spot (so they reopen exactly as written; the drafts themselves are saved in your Gmail).
  • If you add a Claude API key, Spot stores it encrypted with the macOS Keychain.
  • If you turn on Record activity, Spot keeps an activity log on your Mac: which app was in front and the title of its window, with times, recorded only while Spot runs. For apps and window titles on your ignore lists it keeps only the app's name. Next to the log it keeps the names it gives blocks of your activity, and your daily recaps. Each day's files are deleted after 30 days. If you choose a shared activity folder in Spot's Settings, so that your other Macs can include their activity in one recap, these files are kept in that folder instead, and go wherever that folder syncs (for example iCloud Drive). Spot itself sends them nowhere else.
  • Attachments are saved only when you save them, to a place you choose.

What Spot shares

  • Spot sends your data only to Google, and, if you turn on Claude, to Anthropic as described below, to carry out what you ask. It has no analytics, ads or tracking.
  • Anthropic (Claude): if you add your own Claude API key and turn on Use Claude, Spot sends Anthropic what the daily work recap needs, for the day being recapped: app names and window titles from the activity log, with their times (titles on your ignore lists left out), the names, groups and notes of your activity blocks, including time you add yourself, the group names used in the past week, the titles and times of that day's calendar events, and the subjects and recipient names of mail you sent that day. Message bodies are never sent, except for reply drafts and rewrites, ⌃⌥⇧⌘S summaries, appointments and deadlines, and emails you ask to turn into an event with ⌃⌥⇧⌘E (below). Spot sends this about every 2 hours while it runs, and when the recap is written. Under Anthropic's API terms this data is not used to train its models, and Anthropic keeps it for up to 30 days.
  • Drafts and rewrites with ⌘J. When you press ⌘J in a reply, forward or new email in a personal Gmail account, or pick a time or ask for another one on the card of an email that proposes a time, Spot sends Anthropic your notes (from a card, a line Spot writes for the time you chose), the email's recipients, today's date, your reply-style note and your signature, and for a new email its subject. To rewrite text you selected, it sends the email's text as it stands, with the selection marked, and the instruction you typed (for example "shorter"). For a reply or forward it also sends that thread's messages (each with its sender, recipients and date, without their quoted parts, cut to about the last 8,000 tokens) and the names of their attachments (not the files). Nothing is sent from Google Workspace accounts, and nothing is sent until you press ⌘J (and ↩ for a rewrite) or one of those buttons. The draft or rewrite comes back to Spot and is never sent without you. As with the recap, Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models and keeps it for up to 30 days.
  • Plain-English search with ⌘J. When you press ⌘J in the search box, Spot sends the words you typed there and today's date to Anthropic, which turns them into a Gmail search. Nothing from your mailbox is sent: no email, address, contact or label. The search itself then runs in Gmail, as any search does. Anthropic doesn't use this to train its models and keeps it for up to 30 days.
  • Thread summaries with ⌃⌥⇧⌘S. When you press ⌃⌥⇧⌘S on a thread in a personal Gmail account, Spot sends that thread's subject and its messages with their senders, recipients and dates, without quoted parts (about the last 30,000 tokens), and the names of their attachments. Nothing is sent from Google Workspace accounts. Spot keeps the summary only in memory while it runs. As with the recap, Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models and keeps it for up to 30 days.
  • Appointments and deadlines. If you turn this on in Settings, Spot checks each new email on your Mac for a date with a time, or a deadline. Only an email that looks like an appointment or a deadline is sent to Anthropic: from a personal Gmail account, its subject, sender, date, and text without quoted parts (up to about 4,000 tokens); from a Google Workspace account, only its subject and the lines around the date and time, and any line that looks like an address, with email addresses, phone numbers and long ID numbers removed. Spot also sends today's date and your time zone. Anthropic returns the appointment's details, which Spot adds to that account's calendar or offers to, as you choose. Emails with a calendar invitation, mail you sent and newsletters are never sent by this check. You can also press ⌃⌥⇧⌘E on an email to turn it into an event, whatever this setting says: Spot then sends that one email the same way, even mail you sent or a newsletter (never an invitation). As with the recap, Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models and keeps it for up to 30 days.
  • If you choose a daily notes folder, Spot writes each day's recap into that day's note there. It is a file on your Mac, and goes wherever that folder syncs.
  • When you choose Open in Notion Calendar, Spot passes that event's title, time, calendar ID (iCalUID) and account address to the Notion Calendar app on your Mac, which opens the event. Nothing else leaves Spot for any app or service other than Google and Anthropic.
  • Drafts you write in Spot are saved to your Gmail Drafts, like drafts written in Gmail.
  • Emails you schedule wait in your Gmail Drafts; Spot sends them to Google at the time you chose, and only while it is running.
  • Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. It is shared only as described here.
  • Remote images in emails are blocked by default. If you turn them on, opening a message loads its images from the sender's servers, as in any email client.

Limited Use

Spot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data Spot sends to Anthropic (calendar event titles and times, and the subjects and recipient names of mail you sent; for reply drafts, rewrites and thread summaries, a thread's messages with their senders, recipients and dates, and attachment names, and for summaries the thread's subject; for appointments and deadlines, an email's subject, sender, date and text, or for Workspace accounts an excerpt as above) is sent only for the daily recap you turned on, the drafts and rewrites you ask for with ⌘J or a card's buttons, the summaries you ask for with ⌃⌥⇧⌘S, appointments and deadlines if you turn them on, and the emails you ask to turn into an event with ⌃⌥⇧⌘E, and is not used to train AI models.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in Spot's Settings to revoke its access token.
  • Turn off Record activity in Settings to stop recording; delete ~/Library/Application Support/Spot/activity, and the shared activity folder if you chose one, to remove the log at once.
  • Delete ~/Library/Application Support/Spot to remove everything Spot stored on your Mac.
  • You can also remove Spot's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
